Interpretation: the mouse NCLE estimates are positive, but all reported
NCLE confidence intervals include 1 and all P-values exceed 0.05. These data
do not establish a positive association; they also do not exclude a moderate
positive effect. NCLE status does not directly measure misfolding.

Main coding matches human: any detected native non-covalent entanglement.
Only protein length is adjusted in Model 1. No disorder or complex filter.

Additional local-quality check excludes 12 Low-only proteins from primary
(N=776) and 21 from sensitivity (N=1,281). Adjusted NCLE ORs are 1.499071
(0.958901-2.343530; P=0.075754) and 1.393900
(0.904876-2.147209; P=0.131932), respectively.

Quality: mean pLDDT >=70, calculated from exact-v6 residue confidence arrays.
API summary scores are not used for cutoff selection. Q5SUF2 and Q9CXG3 have
API summary scores of 70.0 but residue means 69.974583 and 69.982988.
Both fail the whole-protein arithmetic mean >=70 criterion.

Exact accessions are preserved. 854 experimental rows lack a native archive
match, including all 373 experimental isoform IDs; no remapping is attempted.
Empty detection files are treated as no detected NCLE under the supplied
schema. Original detector completion logs were not supplied.
